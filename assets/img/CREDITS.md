# VESPRA concept illustrations

The files in `vespra/` are original generated artwork made for this fictional
PeakVision concept. The three bottle illustrations use the approved Nocturne,
Reserva, and Primera shapes. They are separate still images, not frames from a
3D model. The earlier Wikimedia images above remain in the repository but are
not used by the current story page.
